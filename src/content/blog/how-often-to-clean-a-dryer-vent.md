## How often to clean a dryer vent at a glance

Clean your dryer vent at least once a year. Clean it about every 6 months if you have pets, a large household, back-to-back loads, or a long vent run. Clean it right away if you notice warning signs, no matter when the last cleaning was.

| Household situation | Suggested interval |
|---|---|
| Typical household with normal loads | At least once a year |
| Large household or back-to-back loads | About every 6 months |
| Pets that shed | About every 6 months |
| Bulky fabrics or many new clothes | Sooner than once a year |
| Long, bent, or attic-routed vent run | About every 6 months |
| Lightly used dryer or second home | Can stretch beyond a year. Ask a technician. |
| New home or unknown vent history | Clean once before regular use, then set a schedule |
| Any warning sign | Clean now |

## Why does advice on how often to clean a dryer vent vary?

Advice varies because dryer use varies. The intervals in circulation run from every 6 months to every 2 years.

Whirlpool's product help says the vent <a href="https://producthelp.whirlpool.com/Laundry/Dryers/Product_Info/Dryer_Product_Assistance/How_to_Check_Venting" rel="nofollow" target="_blank">must be cleaned every one to two years</a>, or sooner when drying performance drops. Whirlpool's dryer vent blog says <a href="https://www.whirlpool.com/blog/washers-and-dryers/how-to-clean-a-dryer-vent.html" rel="nofollow" target="_blank">no less than once every two years</a>.

Many professional sources say at least once a year. Massachusetts fire safety guidance lists <a href="https://www.mass.gov/info-details/dryer-fire-safety" rel="nofollow" target="_blank">cleaning the outside vents twice a year</a>.

A yearly cleaning covers the manufacturer range and the most common professional advice. Shorter intervals apply when use is heavy.

## What shortens the time between dryer vent cleanings?

Five factors move the interval toward 6 months. Each one adds lint to the duct faster or makes lint harder to clear.

### Large households and heavy laundry loads

More loads mean more lint moving through the duct. Households that run the dryer several times a day should clean the vent about every 6 months.

### Pets that shed

Pet hair and fur add to the lint in every load. Vents in homes with shedding pets clog much faster than vents in pet-free homes.

### Bulky items like towels, blankets, and fleece

Bulky fabrics shed more lint per load. A household that dries these items often should clean the vent sooner than once a year.

### New clothes

New clothes produce more lint than clothes that have been washed many times. A wardrobe refresh can speed up buildup for a few months.

### Long or bent vent runs

Added length and elbows reduce airflow and encourage lint buildup, according to Whirlpool. Long runs collect lint faster and restrict airflow sooner than short, direct runs. See [dryer vent length and routing](/dryer-vent-length-and-routing/) for the 35-foot code limit and how bends count against it.

## What lengthens the time between cleanings?

A dryer that runs a few loads a month puts far less lint through the duct. Some vent cleaning specialists say lightly used dryers, such as those in second homes, can go longer than three years between cleanings.

Ask a technician before you stretch the interval.

## Signs your dryer vent needs cleaning before the schedule says so

Clean the vent now if dry times grow, the dryer runs hot, or you smell burning, whatever the calendar says. The [signs of a clogged dryer vent](/signs-of-a-clogged-dryer-vent/) are covered in a separate guide, including the airflow check at the exterior hood.

One quick test uses the timed-dry setting. If a normal 60-minute cycle no longer dries the load, airflow may be restricted.

A burning smell means stop the dryer and unplug it. If you see smoke or flames, get everyone out of the house and call 911 first.

## What to do between dryer vent cleanings

Routine care slows lint buildup but does not replace a full cleaning.

<img

- **After every load:** clean the lint screen.
- **Now and then:** clean the residue off the screen. Fabric softener leaves a film that is usually not visible and can slow drying, according to <a href="https://producthelp.whirlpool.com/Laundry/Dryers/Product_Info/Dryer_Product_Assistance/How_to_Check_Venting" rel="nofollow" target="_blank">Whirlpool</a>.
- **Every 2 to 3 months:** check the exterior hood. The flap should be free, and air should flow out when the dryer runs.
- **Always:** keep the hood at least 12 inches from the ground and from plants, rocks, or snow, per Whirlpool's venting guidance.

## Why does cleaning the lint screen not replace vent cleaning?

The lint screen catches some lint on each cycle, not all of it. The rest passes into the duct and settles on the walls and bends.

Regular screen cleaning slows the buildup but does not stop it. Only a full vent cleaning removes the lint in the duct.

## What does a full dryer vent cleaning cover?

A full cleaning clears the whole path from the dryer to the outside, not just the screen. A complete cleaning includes:

- The lint trap housing
- The duct behind the dryer
- The run through the wall or attic
- The exterior hood

## When should you clean the vent in a new home or with a new dryer?

Clean the vent before regular use if its history is unknown. A new dryer connects to the old vent line, and that line can already be clogged.

Moving into a house with an unknown maintenance history is a good reason to schedule a cleaning. After that cleaning, start your yearly schedule.

## Who is responsible for dryer vent cleaning in a rental?

Check your lease for who handles vent maintenance. If you notice warning signs, tell your landlord or property manager.

Do not disconnect ducts in a rental yourself.

## What should you do if you have never cleaned your dryer vent?

Schedule a cleaning now. You are not unusual.

A home insurer's survey found most homeowners wait two or more years before cleaning the dryer exhaust vent, if they clean it at all. Count your schedule from the day of the first cleaning.

## What does your dryer manual say about vent cleaning?

Your dryer's manual or warranty may list vent cleaning requirements. Read it before you stretch the interval.

## Why should a professional handle gas dryer vent cleaning?

Cleaning the duct from the dryer side means moving the dryer. On a gas dryer, that means working around the gas line.

Leave disconnecting a gas dryer to a professional. If you smell gas, leave the house and call the gas company or 911.
