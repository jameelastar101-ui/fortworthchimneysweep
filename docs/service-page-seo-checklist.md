# Service Page SEO Checklist (Chimney Sweep Pro)

Applies to **service pages only** on fortworthchimneysweep.com. Blog posts, video, Google Business Profile, and lead forms are out of scope.

**Section 6 (Trust and Proof / E-E-A-T) is intentionally omitted** because it is handled by AI. Section numbers are kept as approved so the "per Section X" references still work.

## How to use

For each item, mark **Pass**, **Fail**, or **Can't verify**. Fix every Fail. Anything marked Can't verify goes to the person who owns it.

## Section 1: Targeting and Search Intent

* \[ ] **One primary keyword per page**, in "service + city" form (e.g., "chimney relining Fort Worth"). No two pages target the same keyword.
* \[ ] **Secondary keywords chosen** (2-4 close variants), used naturally and not forced
* \[ ] **Intent confirmed** by checking the live SERP: transactional, or transactional with an informational layer (cost, repair vs. rebuild). The page covers both if the SERP does.
* \[ ] **SERP competitors reviewed** (top 5-10): note their pricing, word count, FAQ, and trust signals, and what they are missing
* \[ ] **Gaps identified and noted** in the SERP snapshot (pricing, process, visuals, permits, insurance angle)
* \[ ] **No cannibalization**: no existing page on the site already targets this keyword or a near-duplicate
* \[ ] **Difficulty tier noted** (easy / moderate / hard). It sets the ranking timeline only. Word count is fixed in Section 3.
* \[ ] **Page is genuinely unique**: not a copy of another city or service page with swapped names
* \[ ] **USP defined** in one sentence, provable, and chosen from what the SERP already shows and rewards
* \[ ] **UVP defined** in one sentence as a customer outcome tied to the search intent
* \[ ] **USP appears in the title tag or meta description**
* \[ ] **UVP appears in the H1 or hero subheading** (above the fold)
* \[ ] **No generic claims** ("best," "quality service," "trusted experts") without evidence

## Section 2: Title Tag, Meta Description, URL, H1

### Title tag

* \[ ] **Primary keyword near the front**, in "service + city" form
* \[ ] **ALL CAPS** (e.g., `CHIMNEY REBUILD FORT WORTH, TX | COST GUIDE`)
* \[ ] **Width under 580px** on desktop, checked in a SERP preview tool (characters are only a rough guide)
* \[ ] **Fits the pixel limit in caps**: capitals are wider than lowercase, so expect roughly 40-45 characters
* \[ ] **One short differentiator** after the keyword: the USP or a price signal (e.g., "COST GUIDE," "$950-$2,500")
* \[ ] **No free offer wording** ("Free Quote," "Free Estimate")
* \[ ] **State abbreviation included** (TX)
* \[ ] **Unique across the site**
* \[ ] **Written for the click**: reads like a human benefit, not a keyword string
* \[ ] **No keyword stuffing**, no repeated city or service words
* \[ ] **Brand name at the end** only if there is room within the pixel limit

### Meta description

* \[ ] **Width under 920px on desktop and 680px on mobile**, checked in a SERP preview tool
* \[ ] **Primary keyword and city included** naturally
* \[ ] **No free offer wording**
* \[ ] **Ends with the call CTA**: "Call (877) 203-8193"

### URL

* \[ ] **Existing live URL used** for every service already on the site. Do not rewrite it.
* \[ ] **New slug only when adding a new service**, following the live pattern: short, lowercase, hyphen-separated, primary keyword + city + tx (e.g., `/chimney-rebuild-fort-worth-tx/`)
* \[ ] **Flat structure**: no parent folders (no `/services/`), applied to all pages
* \[ ] **No dates, IDs, parameters, or stop words** in new slugs
* \[ ] **Permanent**: no changes after publishing unless a 301 redirect is set up
* \[ ] **Canonical tag** points to the page's own URL

### H1

* \[ ] **Exactly one H1** on the page
* \[ ] **Contains the primary keyword and city** naturally
* \[ ] **Differs slightly from the title tag** but matches the same intent
* \[ ] **Carries the UVP**, or is paired with a subheading that does (per Section 1)
* \[ ] **Readable at a glance on mobile** (wraps to 2-3 lines at most)
* \[ ] **Visible above the fold**, not hidden in an image or slider
* \[ ] **No free offer wording**

### Heading hierarchy

* \[ ] **H2s follow the H1** and mirror real searcher questions or steps. H3s nest only under H2s, with no skipped levels.

## Section 3: Content Structure and Copy

### Page structure (top to bottom)

* \[ ] **Hero above the fold**: H1, UVP subheading, and the call CTA "Call (877) 203-8193"
* \[ ] **Opening paragraph (first 100 words)** states the service, the city, and the USP, and mentions the problems the service solves
* \[ ] **Problem/symptom section**: warning signs the searcher recognizes
* \[ ] **Service explanation**: what the service is, what it includes, and when it is needed
* \[ ] **Pricing section** (per Section 4)
* \[ ] **Decision framework**: the choice the searcher is making (repair vs. rebuild, coating vs. rebuild, Level 1/2/3, reline vs. patch)
* \[ ] **Mid-page call CTA** after the pricing or decision section
* \[ ] **Process section**: numbered steps of the visit, including what happens if a problem is found
* \[ ] **Local section**: Fort Worth-specific causes and conditions (per Section 5)
* \[ ] **FAQ block** (per Section 8)
* \[ ] **Closing CTA**: call only, with a short reason to call now. No lead form.
* \[ ] **H2 count is flexible**: no minimum or maximum

### Copy rules

* \[ ] **Primary keyword and city** used in the H1, the first 100 words, at least one H2, and the closing section, naturally
* \[ ] **Secondary keywords** and related terms spread across H2s and body copy
* \[ ] **Answer first**: each H2 opens with a direct answer in 1-2 sentences, then adds detail
* \[ ] **Short paragraphs**: 2-4 sentences, one idea each
* \[ ] **Plain language**: technical terms (flashing, crown, liner) explained on first use
* \[ ] **Scannable formatting**: descriptive H2/H3s, bullets for lists, a table for comparisons where it helps
* \[ ] **Specific over generic**: real numbers, materials, code references, and named neighborhoods
* \[ ] **No generic claims** ("best," "quality service," "trusted experts") without evidence (per Section 1)
* \[ ] **No free offer wording** anywhere on the page (per Section 2)
* \[ ] **Written for the searcher**, second person ("your chimney"), no marketing filler

### Length

* \[ ] **Minimum 1,200 words** of body copy (pricing, decision content, and FAQ count toward it)

### Content coverage

* \[ ] **Covers every topic the top-ranking pages cover**, plus the chosen gap angle
* \[ ] **Answers the "People Also Ask" questions** for the keyword, in the body or the FAQ
* \[ ] **Service scope stated clearly**: what is included and what is not
* \[ ] **LSI and semantic terms included**: related terms and synonyms a topic expert would use (flue liner, crown, flashing, damper, creosote, mortar joints, stainless steel liner), used naturally
* \[ ] **Entities covered**: CSIA, NFPA 211, Fort Worth Building Inspection Division, Tarrant County, named neighborhoods, materials, and chimney components
* \[ ] **Entities defined on first mention** and named consistently afterward (same term for the same thing)
* \[ ] **Relationships between entities stated explicitly** (e.g., "a damaged crown lets water into the flue, which..."), not implied

### Helping AI understand the page

* \[ ] **Clear subject in every section**: each H2/H3 names what the section is about, so it makes sense out of context
* \[ ] **Direct, self-contained answers** an AI can quote without the surrounding text
* \[ ] **Explicit facts in plain sentences**: service, city, price, process, and warranty stated as text, not only in images, sliders, or tabs
* \[ ] **Structured elements used**: tables for comparisons, numbered lists for steps, bullets for symptoms and inclusions
* \[ ] **Terminology consistent** with the schema, title, and H1 (same service name and city everywhere)
* \[ ] **Business identity stated in text**: business name, service area, and phone number appear in the body, matching the schema

### Consistency with the migration brief

* \[ ] **Uses the shared components** (Hero, WhyChooseUs, ServiceAreas, FAQ) so the copy fits the layout. `LeadForm` is not used on service pages.
* \[ ] **Frontmatter fields filled** (title, metaDescription, ogImage, phone) for the page's existing slug

## Section 4: Pricing and Decision-Making Content

### Pricing

* \[ ] **Price shown as text on the page**: a range or "starting at" figure, not hidden in an image, tab, or form
* \[ ] **Pricing is mandatory on every page**: no page ships without a range or starting price
* \[ ] **Price is real and current**: matches what the business actually charges, and is reviewed on a set schedule
* \[ ] **Cost factors listed**: what moves the price up or down (chimney height, access, materials, damage extent, liner type)
* \[ ] **Price tiers by type or scope** where the service has options (e.g., crown coating vs. full crown rebuild, flexible vs. rigid liner)
* \[ ] **What is included stated**: inspection, materials, cleanup, permits, warranty
* \[ ] **What is not included stated**: extras that could raise the final cost
* \[ ] **Price format consistent** across the page, title/meta (if used), FAQ, and schema (e.g., "$950-$2,500")
* \[ ] **How the final quote works**: when the number is fixed, and whether it is given in writing before work starts
* \[ ] **Price appears in a table or a short answer-first sentence** so it can be scanned and quoted
* \[ ] **No "last updated" date** shown next to the price

### Decision-making content

* \[ ] **The core decision is named**: the choice the searcher faces (repair vs. rebuild, coating vs. rebuild, Level 1/2/3, reline vs. patch)
* \[ ] **Decision framework included**: a table or checklist with clear thresholds (e.g., "if damage exceeds X, rebuild")
* \[ ] **Signs that point to each option** listed side by side
* \[ ] **Cost of waiting explained**: what happens if the problem is ignored (water damage, safety risk, higher repair cost)
* \[ ] **Urgency levels separated**: emergency, soon, and can wait
* \[ ] **DIY vs. professional guidance**: when DIY is reasonable and when it is not
* \[ ] **Insurance angle covered**: when insurance may cover the work (storm, hail, sudden damage), what documentation is provided, and what typically is not covered
* \[ ] **Permit and code requirements noted** where they apply (Fort Worth Building Inspection Division, HOA, historic district)
* \[ ] **Clear next step**: the decision content ends with the call CTA "Call (877) 203-8193"

### Consistency

* \[ ] **Wording follows Section 3 rules**: answer first, plain language, no generic claims, no free offer wording
* \[ ] **Uses the same entity names** as the rest of the page

## Section 5: Local Signals

### Page-wide readability

* \[ ] **Reading level of grade 10-11** (Flesch-Kincaid Grade Level) across the whole page, checked with a readability tool before publishing
* \[ ] **Technical terms explained** in plain words on first use
* \[ ] **Sentence length kept moderate**: average about 15-20 words, splitting any sentence that runs long

### Local content

* \[ ] **City named naturally** in the H1, first 100 words, at least one H2, and the closing section, with no forced repetition
* \[ ] **Fort Worth-specific causes stated**: expansive clay soil, freeze-thaw cycles, hail and storm exposure, summer heat and thermal cycling
* \[ ] **Local housing stock referenced**: pre-1950 lime-mortar homes vs. newer builds, and how that changes the work
* \[ ] **Named neighborhoods tied to a technical point**, not only listed (e.g., Fairmount's older masonry vs. newer construction in Benbrook). The number of neighborhoods is flexible.
* \[ ] **Local codes and permits mentioned** where they apply: Fort Worth Building Inspection Division, Tarrant County, HOA, and historic-district approvals
* \[ ] **Local facts verified**: no wrong or generic details (no "tropical climate," no wrong state)
* \[ ] **Local content unique to the page**: not the same sentence pattern with neighborhood names swapped

### Service area

* \[ ] **Service area stated in text** using the ServiceAreas component
* \[ ] **ZIP codes: one verified master list**, shown by the ServiceAreas component on every page. Body copy mentions only the neighborhoods relevant to that service.
* \[ ] **Only areas actually served** are listed
* \[ ] **Nearby cities included** where the business serves them (e.g., Keller, Saginaw)

### NAP (name, address, phone)

* \[ ] **Business name, address, and phone** appear in the page body or footer as text
* \[ ] **Phone number is (877) 203-8193** everywhere: header CTA, hero, footer, click-to-call links, and schema. No trace of (877) 203-8193.
* \[ ] **NAP matches exactly** across the site and schema (same spelling, abbreviations, and formatting)
* \[ ] **Business address**: 804 Mustang Dr, Saginaw, TX 76179

### Local schema and maps

* \[ ] **LocalBusiness schema** in the main layout with name, address, phone, hours, geo coordinates, and service area
* \[ ] **Service area in schema** matches the service area stated on the page
* \[ ] **Embedded map or a link** where it fits the design

## Section 7: Media

### Images

* \[ ] **Real photos of the business's work**, not stock images
* \[ ] **Descriptive file names**, lowercase and hyphenated (e.g., `chimney-crown-repair-fort-worth.webp`)
* \[ ] **Modern format** (WebP or AVIF), compressed without visible quality loss
* \[ ] **Sized correctly**: no image larger than its display size, with width and height attributes set to prevent layout shift
* \[ ] **Hero image not lazy-loaded**, and all other images lazy-loaded
* \[ ] **Images that get redirected to** are placed in `public/` at a fixed path, not run through `astro:assets`
* \[ ] **No minimum image count**: use as many images as the page needs

### Alt text

* \[ ] **Written fresh for each image**: describes what the image actually shows
* \[ ] **No empty alt attributes** on content images (decorative images only may use `alt=""`)
* \[ ] **Service and city included only where they fit naturally**, with no keyword stuffing
* \[ ] **Under about 125 characters**
* \[ ] Alt of **Hero image is same as Title**

### Captions and context

* \[ ] **Captions on before/after photos**: neighborhood, problem, work done
* \[ ] **Text near each image supports it**, so the image and copy reinforce the same topic

### Icons and graphics

* \[ ] **Flat two-tone icons** matching the design direction (red/white or black/white)
* \[ ] **Diagrams or tables as real text or SVG** where possible, not flattened into images

### Image search

* \[ ] **Images listed in the image sitemap** with the new URLs

## Section 8: FAQ

### Question count and mix

* \[ ] **Minimum 8 questions**, no maximum
* \[ ] **2 company questions** focused on the USP and UVP
* \[ ] **2 service questions** about the specific service: what it includes, how it works, how long it takes
* \[ ] **2 location questions** tied to Fort Worth: local conditions, service area, or local codes and permits
* \[ ] **Remaining questions from "People Also Ask"** and related searches for the primary keyword
* \[ ] **No yes/no questions**: start with "How," "What," "Why," "When," or "Which," not "Is," "Can," "Do," or "Does"
* \[ ] **No answers that begin with "Yes" or "No"**

### Question selection

* \[ ] **Questions phrased the way people search**, in plain language
* \[ ] **No duplicate questions**, and none repeats a full section already on the page
* \[ ] **Service and city included** in some questions where natural (e.g., "How much does chimney relining cost in Fort Worth?")
* \[ ] **PAA questions rewritten** into non-yes/no form where needed, keeping the searcher's intent

### Answer writing

* \[ ] **Answer first**: the direct answer in the opening sentence, then a short supporting detail
* \[ ] **Answer length of 40-60 words**
* \[ ] **Self-contained answers** that make sense without the surrounding page
* \[ ] **Specific facts**: real prices, timeframes, standards, and names
* \[ ] **Price answers match** the pricing section and schema exactly
* \[ ] **Grade 10-11 reading level** applies to FAQ answers too
* \[ ] **No generic claims** without evidence
* \[ ] **No free offer wording**
* \[ ] **Call CTA "Call (877) 203-8193"** on urgent or cost answers only, not every answer

### Placement and format

* \[ ] **FAQ block placed near the end** of the page, before the closing CTA
* \[ ] **Uses the FAQ component** (native `<details>/<summary>` accordion)
* \[ ] **Answers present in the page HTML**, so they are crawlable even when collapsed
* \[ ] **Descriptive H2 for the block** (e.g., "Chimney Relining FAQs for Fort Worth Homeowners")

### FAQ schema

* \[ ] **FAQPage schema generated from the same FAQ data** that renders the accordion, not typed separately
* \[ ] **Schema text matches visible text** exactly
* \[ ] **Schema only on pages with a visible FAQ**
* \[ ] **Validated in Google's Rich Results Test** for one page per template type

### Consistency

* \[ ] **Entities and terms consistent** with the rest of the page
* \[ ] **FAQ words count toward the 1,200-word minimum**

* \[ ] **No marked-up content that is not visible** on the page
* \[ ] **No duplicate schema blocks** of the same type on one page
* \[ ] **No generic claims or free offer wording** in schema descriptions
* \[ ] **Price and phone formats identical** across schema, title/meta, and body
* \[ ] **No `HowTo` schema**

### Validation

* \[ ] **Validated in Google's Rich Results Test and the Schema Markup Validator** for one page per service page template (and the homepage for LocalBusiness)

## Section 10: Internal Linking

### Links out of the service page

* \[ ] **Link to related service pages** where the topic connects, using the existing live URLs (e.g., cap, leak repair, repair; inspection, repair, relining)
* \[ ] **Link to the parent service landing page** (`/chimney-sweep-services/`) where it fits naturally
* \[ ] **Link to related informational pages** on the site (cost or symptom articles) where they answer a question raised on the page
* \[ ] **Closing CTA is call only**: a click-to-call link (`tel:`) with (877) 203-8193, and no Contact page link or form beside it
* \[ ] **No links to competitors** or low-quality external sites. External links only to authoritative sources (e.g., NFPA, CSIA) where they add value.

### Links into the service page

* \[ ] **Homepage links to the page** through the ServiceGrid component
* \[ ] **Header "Our Services" dropdown and footer service links** include the page
* \[ ] **Related pages link back** to this page with descriptive anchor text
* \[ ] **No orphan pages**: every service page is reachable within 2 clicks from the homepage

### Anchor text

* \[ ] **Descriptive anchors** that say what the destination is (e.g., "chimney crown repair"), never "click here" or "read more"
* \[ ] **Varied anchors**: a mix of exact-match, partial-match, and natural phrases, with no identical anchor repeated on every page
* \[ ] **Anchors match the destination's topic** and primary keyword, without stuffing
* \[ ] **Anchor text is readable** at grade 10-11 and fits the sentence naturally

### Placement and quantity

* \[ ] **Links placed in body copy** where the reader would want them, not only in footers or sidebars
* \[ ] **Number of internal links is flexible**: no minimum or maximum, as long as each link is relevant and useful
* \[ ] **No more than one link to the same URL** per section
* \[ ] **Links open in the same tab**, except external links, which may open in a new tab with `rel="noopener"`
* \[ ] **Click-to-call links** use the `tel:` format with (877) 203-8193

### Link health

* \[ ] **All internal links use the exact live URL**, including the trailing slash, so nothing triggers a redirect
* \[ ] **No broken links** (checked before publishing and after launch)
* \[ ] **No links to redirected or removed URLs**
* \[ ] **New services added to the linking map**: when a new service page is created, update related pages to link to it

### Hub-and-spoke structure

* \[ ] **Related services grouped into clusters** (e.g., water intrusion: leak repair, crown, flashing, cap), with each page linking to its cluster neighbors that have pages
* \[ ] **Cluster map documented** so each new page knows which pages it must link to and receive links from

## Section 11: Technical and Mobile

### Indexing and canonicals

* \[ ] **Self-referencing canonical tag** pointing to the exact live URL with the trailing slash
* \[ ] **Page is indexable**: no `noindex` tag, and not blocked in `robots.txt`
* \[ ] **Page included in the sitemap** (`@astrojs/sitemap`), and the sitemap is submitted to Google Search Console
* \[ ] **One URL version only**: HTTPS, and www or non-www applied consistently, with the other redirecting
* \[ ] **Existing live URL preserved**: the slug matches the inventory exactly

### Open Graph and social tags

* \[ ] **`og:title`, `og:description`, `og:image`, and `og:type`** filled per page from the new content
* \[ ] **`og:image` set** to a real image for the page (`ogImage` frontmatter), at 1200×630
* \[ ] **`og:url` matches** the canonical URL

### Page speed and Core Web Vitals

* \[ ] **LCP under 2.5 seconds** on mobile, with the hero image optimized and not lazy-loaded
* \[ ] **CLS under 0.1**: image dimensions set, no late-loading elements that shift layout
* \[ ] **INP under 200 ms**: minimal JavaScript, no jQuery or heavy plugins
* \[ ] **Fonts optimized**: only the needed weights, `font-display: swap`, preloaded where it helps
* \[ ] **CSS and JavaScript minimized** by the Astro build, with no unused libraries
* \[ ] **Static output**: prebuilt HTML with no server rendering
* \[ ] **Tested with PageSpeed Insights or Lighthouse** on mobile before publishing

### Mobile

* \[ ] **Responsive layout** with the viewport meta tag set, and no horizontal scrolling
* \[ ] **Tap targets at least 48×48 px**, with enough spacing, especially the call button
* \[ ] **Body text at least 16px**
* \[ ] **Sticky click-to-call button** on mobile that does not cover content
* \[ ] **Tables and comparison content** scroll or reflow cleanly on small screens
* \[ ] **Header nav and "Our Services" dropdown** work by touch
* \[ ] **Tested on real devices** or an emulator, not only a desktop browser

### Accessibility basics

* \[ ] **Color contrast** meets WCAG AA (check white text on brand red `#D72828` and any smaller text)
* \[ ] **Semantic HTML**: one `<main>`, landmarks for header, nav, and footer, headings in order
* \[ ] **Keyboard usable**: the accordion, dropdown, and call button work without a mouse, with visible focus states
* \[ ] **`lang="en"`** set on the page

### Crawl and security

* \[ ] **HTTPS across the whole page**, with no mixed content
* \[ ] **No broken links, images, or scripts**
* \[ ] **Clean status code**: the page returns 200, with no redirect chains
* \[ ] **`robots.txt` allows all** and points to the new sitemap

### Redirects

* \[ ] **301 redirects only for URLs outside the inventory** that still get traffic, set in `netlify.toml` or `\_redirects`
* \[ ] **Indexed image URLs redirect** to their new replacements
