# Blog Post SEO Checklist (Chimney Sweep Pro)

Applies to **blog posts only** on fortworthchimneysweep.com. Service pages, video, and Google Business Profile are out of scope.

## How to use

For each item, mark **Pass**, **Fail**, or **Can't verify**. Fix every Fail. Anything marked Can't verify goes to the person who owns it.

|Sections|Main owner|
|-|-|
|1-6, 8 (content)|Claude in chat, using the blog writing rules file|
|7 (media)|You supply images; Claude writes alt text for uploaded images|
|9-10 (schema, technical)|Claude Code build, checked by you|

## Section 1: Targeting and Search Intent

* \[ ] **One primary keyword per post**, informational in form (e.g., "black chimney smoke"). No two posts target the same keyword.
* \[ ] **Secondary keywords chosen** (2-4 close variants), used naturally and not forced
* \[ ] **Intent confirmed** by checking the live SERP: how-to, cause and diagnosis, cost guide, comparison, or explainer
* \[ ] **SERP competitors reviewed** (top 5-10): note the subtopics they cover, their length, local signals, and what they are missing
* \[ ] **No cannibalization** between posts, or with a service page: the post answers the question and hands the reader to the service page for the transactional step
* \[ ] **Existing live URL used**, or a new slug only when adding a new post

\---

## Section 2: Title Tag, Meta Description, URL, H1

### Title tag

* \[ ] **Primary keyword near the front**
* \[ ] **ALL CAPS** (e.g., `BLACK CHIMNEY SMOKE: CAUSES \& FIXES`)
* \[ ] **Width under 580px** on desktop, checked in a SERP preview tool (characters are only a rough guide, about 40-45 in caps)
* \[ ] **"FORT WORTH" included** where it fits within the pixel limit. If not, the city appears in the meta, H1, and opening.
* \[ ] **One short differentiator** (e.g., "CAUSES \& FIXES," "COST FACTORS"), with no price
* \[ ] **No free offer wording**
* \[ ] **Unique across the site**, with no keyword stuffing or repeated words

### Meta description

* \[ ] **Width under 920px on desktop and 680px on mobile**, checked in a SERP preview tool
* \[ ] **Primary keyword included** naturally, and the city where it fits
* \[ ] **No free offer wording and no prices**
* \[ ] **Ends with a short call to action** matching the CTA chosen for the post (e.g., "Call (682) 354-6644")

### URL and H1

* \[ ] **Slug matches the live inventory** exactly, with the trailing slash, and the canonical points to it
* \[ ] **Flat structure**: no `/blog/` or other parent folders
* \[ ] **Exactly one H1**, in sentence case, containing the primary keyword
* \[ ] **H1 differs slightly from the title tag** and matches the same intent
* \[ ] **Heading order has no skipped levels**, and headings name their subject

\---

## Section 3: Content Coverage and No Repetition

### Coverage

* \[ ] **Opening paragraph (first 100 words)** answers the question directly, names the city, and states what the post covers
* \[ ] **Short answer of 40-60 words** placed in the opening or directly under the first H2, quotable on its own
* \[ ] **Covers every subtopic the top-ranking pages cover**
* \[ ] **Adds the adjacent subtopics** the reader needs next (causes, signs, related conditions, prevention, safety, what a professional does), as chosen
* \[ ] **People Also Ask questions answered** as H2/H3 sections with direct 40-60 word answers, phrased so they don't start with Is, Can, Do, Does, Are, Should, or Will
* \[ ] **Structure follows the content type**: numbered steps for how-to, a symptom/cause table for diagnosis, a cost-factors table for cost topics (no dollar amounts), a side-by-side table for comparisons
* \[ ] **Related terms and entities included and defined on first mention**: flue liner, crown, flashing, damper, creosote, mortar joints, CSIA, NFPA 211, and others that fit
* \[ ] **Relationships between entities stated explicitly** ("a cracked crown lets water reach the flue, which...")

### No repeated information

* \[ ] **Each fact appears once**: later mentions refer back by name and do not restate
* \[ ] **Every section adds new information**: deleting any section would lose a fact or decision
* \[ ] **No intro roadmap**, no section summaries, and no closing recap
* \[ ] **Tables not restated in prose**, and prose points not restated in tables
* \[ ] **No FAQ block** that repeats the body
* \[ ] **Same term for the same thing** used throughout (repeating a term is fine, repeating a fact is not)

\---

## Section 4: Length, Readability, and Copy

* \[ ] **Body copy is at least 1,500 words**, reached through subtopic coverage and depth rather than repetition, including tables and headings (not title or meta), checked with a word count tool
* \[ ] **Reading level of grade 10-11** (Flesch-Kincaid Grade Level), checked with a readability tool
* \[ ] **Technical terms explained** in plain words on first use
* \[ ] **Sentence length moderate**: average about 15-20 words, splitting any sentence that runs long
* \[ ] **Answer first**: each H2 opens with a direct answer in 1-2 sentences
* \[ ] **Short paragraphs**: 2-4 sentences, one idea each
* \[ ] **Written for the reader**, second person, no marketing filler
* \[ ] **Specific over generic**: real numbers, materials, code references, and named neighborhoods
* \[ ] **No generic claims** ("best," "quality service," "trusted experts") without evidence
* \[ ] **No free offer wording** anywhere on the page
* \[ ] **Primary keyword and city** in the H1 (where natural), the first 100 words, at least one H2, and the closing section

## Section 5: Local Signals

* \[ ] **Fort Worth-specific causes stated** where they change the answer: expansive clay soil, freeze-thaw cycles, hail and storm exposure, summer heat and thermal cycling
* \[ ] **Local housing stock referenced** where relevant: pre-1950 lime-mortar homes vs. newer builds
* \[ ] **Named neighborhoods tied to a technical point**, not only listed. The number of neighborhoods is flexible.
* \[ ] **Local codes and permits mentioned** where they apply: Fort Worth Building Inspection Division, Tarrant County, HOA, historic district
* \[ ] **Local facts verified**: no wrong or generic details (no "tropical climate," no wrong state)
* \[ ] **Local content unique to the post**: not the same sentence pattern with neighborhood names swapped
* \[ ] **Business name, service area, and phone number** stated in the body text
* \[ ] **No ZIP code lists** in the body
* \[ ] **Phone number is (682) 354-6644** everywhere, with no trace of (877) 203-8193

\---

## Section 6: No Prices, Safety, and CTA

The call-only CTA rule applies to service pages, not blog posts.

### No prices

* \[ ] **No price, dollar amount, price range, or "starting at" figure** anywhere in the post: title, meta, body, tables, or links
* \[ ] **Cost topics explain what drives the cost** (factors, materials, access, extent of damage) and what a job includes, without numbers

### Safety and urgency

* \[ ] **Clear thresholds** for when to call a professional, and what can wait
* \[ ] **DIY vs. professional guidance** where it applies
* \[ ] **Fire, smoke, active chimney fire, or carbon monoxide alarm**: the reader is told to get out and call 911 before calling the business
* \[ ] **Safety statements accurate and conservative**, using only details the user chose

### CTA

* \[ ] **One CTA chosen for the post**: a call link, a link to the matching service page, or a link to `/contact-us/`. No forms.
* \[ ] **No free offer wording** in any CTA
* \[ ] **CTA placed where the reader decides to get help** (the "when to call a professional" section and the closing line), not in every section
* \[ ] **Short reason to act** tied to the topic, with urgency wording only where it applies
* \[ ] **Call links use `tel:+16823546644`**, with the visible text "(682) 354-6644"

## Section 7: Media

* \[ ] **Real photos of the business's work** where used, not stock images
* \[ ] **Descriptive file names**, lowercase and hyphenated
* \[ ] **Modern format** (WebP or AVIF), sized correctly, with width and height attributes set
* \[ ] **Hero image not lazy-loaded**, and all other images lazy-loaded
* \[ ] **Alt text written fresh** for each image, describing what it shows, under about 125 characters, with no "image of" opener and no keyword stuffing
* \[ ] **No empty alt attributes** on content images
* \[ ] **Text near each image supports it**
* \[ ] **Diagrams and tables as real text or SVG** where possible
* \[ ] **Images listed in the image sitemap** with the new URLs

## Section 8: Internal Linking

* \[ ] **Links to the matching service page** where the reader would take the next step, using the exact live URL
* \[ ] **Links to related blog posts** where they answer a question raised in the post
* \[ ] **Contact page link (`/contact-us/`) only if it is the CTA chosen for the post**, and no forms
* \[ ] **Descriptive, varied anchors**, never "click here" or "read more"
* \[ ] **No more than one link to the same URL** per section
* \[ ] **Number of links is flexible**: as many as are relevant and useful
* \[ ] **All internal links use the exact live URL** with the trailing slash, and none is broken
* \[ ] **No links to competitors**. External links only to authoritative sources (e.g., NFPA, CSIA) where they add value.
* \[ ] **Post is linked from related posts and the blog category page**, so it is not an orphan

\---

## Section 9: Schema Markup

* \[ ] **`Article` (or `BlogPosting`) schema** with headline, datePublished, dateModified, author, and publisher, pulled from frontmatter and never hand-typed separately
* \[ ] **Author and publisher set to the business** (Chimney Sweep Pro), with no invented person
* \[ ] **`BreadcrumbList` schema** generated from the URL path and confirmed to render
* \[ ] **LocalBusiness schema** renders sitewide from the main layout, with NAP identical to the page text
* \[ ] **No `FAQPage` schema** unless a visible FAQ exists (none by default)
* \[ ] **No `Review` or `AggregateRating` schema** without real, verifiable review data
* \[ ] **No marked-up content that is not visible**, and no duplicate schema blocks
* \[ ] **Validated in Google's Rich Results Test and the Schema Markup Validator** for one post per template

\---

## Section 10: Technical and Mobile

* \[ ] **Self-referencing canonical tag**, and the page is indexable and in the sitemap
* \[ ] **Open Graph tags filled** (`og:title`, `og:description`, `og:image`, `og:type`, `og:url`), with a real 1200×630 image
* \[ ] **LCP under 2.5 seconds, CLS under 0.1, INP under 200 ms** on mobile, tested in PageSpeed Insights
* \[ ] **Responsive layout**, no horizontal scrolling, body text at least 16px, tap targets at least 48×48 px
* \[ ] **Sticky click-to-call button** on mobile that does not cover content
* \[ ] **Tables scroll or reflow cleanly** on small screens
* \[ ] **Color contrast meets WCAG AA**, semantic HTML, keyboard usable, `lang="en"`
* \[ ] **HTTPS, no mixed content, no broken links, and a clean 200 status**
* \[ ] **301 redirects only for URLs outside the inventory** that still get traffic

## Section 11: Pre-Publish QA and Post-Publish Tracking

### Pre-publish

* \[ ] **Title is ALL CAPS and fits 580px; meta fits 920px desktop and 680px mobile and ends with a call to action**
* \[ ] **Exactly one H1**, URL matches the inventory, and the canonical is self-referencing
* \[ ] **Body is at least 1,500 words** and readability is grade 10-11
* \[ ] **Repetition check passed**: each section adds new information, with no recap
* \[ ] **Subtopics covered** match the chosen list
* \[ ] **Local signals present** and local facts verified
* \[ ] **No prices appear anywhere in the post**
* \[ ] **Safety statements checked** by the business owner
* \[ ] **Every image has fresh alt text**
* \[ ] **Internal links resolve** and point to the matching service page
* \[ ] **CTA matches the one chosen for the post**, and there is no trace of (877) 203-8193
* \[ ] **Schema validates** with no errors
* \[ ] **Grammar and spelling checked** with an AI or grammar tool

### Post-publish

* \[ ] **Request indexing** in Google Search Console for the URL
* \[ ] **Live URL inspection** confirms the content, schema, and canonical render correctly
* \[ ] **Live page re-checked** for broken links, missing images, and mobile layout
* \[ ] **Rankings and Search Console queries reviewed** monthly (impressions, clicks, CTR, average position)
* \[ ] **Clicks to the linked service page and calls tracked** by post
* \[ ] **Title and meta tested** if CTR is low for the position the post holds
* \[ ] **Competitor SERP re-checked** every quarter for new subtopics
* \[ ] **Content refreshed** when local facts or competitors change, and the refresh is logged
